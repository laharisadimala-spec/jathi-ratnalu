## 🛠️ How to Edit Content & Upload Photos

All editable content is centralized in **`src/data/siteConfig.js`**. You don't need to touch any UI components to update your information!

### 1. Uploading Photos
Place your image files in `/public/images/`:
- `hero.jpg` — The main group photo of Lahari, Vedha & Keerthi
- `friend-1.jpg` — Lahari's photo
- `friend-2.jpg` — Vedha's photo
- `friend-3.jpg` — Keerthi's photo
- `jathi-ratnalu.jpg` — Jathi Ratnalu poster
- `moment-1.jpg` to `moment-6.jpg` — The 6 scrapbook memory photos

*While placeholders are active, the website displays stylized vintage cinema graphics so it looks great right now.*

---

### 2. Updating Locations in Friendship Map
In `src/data/siteConfig.js`, you can update `friendshipMap.locations` with your real hometowns or neighborhoods:
- `FRIEND_1_LOCATION` (Lahari's origin)
- `FRIEND_2_LOCATION` (Vedha's origin)
- `FRIEND_3_LOCATION` (Keerthi's origin)
- Destination: Stanley College of Engineering (`STANLEY_COLLEGE_OF_ENGINEERING`)
